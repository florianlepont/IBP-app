<objective>
Stand up the throwaway spike environment, resolve the two human lead-time items (benchmark phones
per D-18, field photographs per D-16), and create the committed measurement document that every
later plan writes into and that the ADR will cite.

Purpose: the spike is timeboxed to two or three days (D-19). Everything in this plan is the kind of
setup that silently eats half a day if it is discovered mid-spike — a missing ANDROID_HOME, a Python
version with no torch wheel, throwaway files tripping the format gate, or a latency measurement that
cannot run because nobody knows which phone to measure on.

Output: a gitignored `spike/species-recognition/` working tree, ignore-file entries, and
`docs/technical/species-recognition-spike-measurements-v1.md` with its full section skeleton.

This phase ships a decision, not a feature. A well-evidenced no-go is a successful outcome. Nothing
in this plan assumes the answer is yes.
</objective>
