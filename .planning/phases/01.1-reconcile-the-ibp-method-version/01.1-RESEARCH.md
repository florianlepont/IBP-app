## Summary

**The app is not v3.2-conformant.** Comparing the rules the app actually runs (API `ibp-rules.service.ts`, mobile `ibp-scoring.ts`, form spec, validation matrix) against the official IBP FR v3.2 PDF (02/02/2026, 28 pp., SHA-256 `f130b3d6…d001`), v3.2 changes the **scoring engine itself in four places** and changes **what the observer counts** in about twenty more:

1. **The native-cover cap belongs to Factor A, not Factor B** (v3.2 p. 3 and p. 20). The app caps **B** at 2 when native cover < 50 %. An official CNPF v3 survey sheet from 2021 also puts the cap on **A**, so this is almost certainly a v3.0 transcription bug that v3.2 does not fix — it is wrong under both versions.
2. **C and D, score 1**: v3.2 tests `(BMm + BMg)/ha ≥ 1`; the app tests `BMm/ha ≥ 1` alone.
3. **E, score 1**: v3.2 tests `(GB + TGB)/ha ≥ 1`; the app tests `GB/ha ≥ 1` alone.
4. **The regional model changed**: v3.0's "region (ACA / M) + vegetation stage" became v3.2's "cas 1 / 2 / 3 / 4". The cas-3 ("subalpine") scales for A and G now apply only to the **middle and upper subalpine** — the lower subalpine is cas 1. They also apply to cas-2 stands in cas-3 zones and to lapiaz, dune, peat-bog and *Juniperus thurifera* habitats. The app applies the subalpine scales to any `ACA` + `subalpin` survey and has no way to record cas 2 or those habitats.

The field-definition changes include:
- **Strata heights (B):** cas 1 is 7–18 m / > 18 m where v3.0 ACA used 7–20 m / > 20 m. Cas 2/3/4 is 5–12 m / > 12 m where v3.0 M used 5–15 m / > 15 m.
- **Diameter thresholds for cas 3 and cas 2 (C/D/E).**
- **Dendromicrohabitat groups (F):** ferns join group 12 and old resin flows join group 15.
- **Open flowering habitats (G):** flowering vegetation in the intermediate and high strata is excluded.
- **Grazing and orchard rules for H.**
- **Factor I:** a new aquatic type, "Mer ou océan".
- **Factor J:** twelve rocky types instead of nine.
- **The Factor A genus list:** Cupressus and Tamarix added, Juniperus widened beyond *J. thurifera*, the five supplementary genera extended to cas 2, and the species-level Table 1 with archaeophytes and every poplar cultivar.

Global aggregation (A–G max 35, H–J max 15, total max 50), all count thresholds, the G percentage bands and the interpretation chart are unchanged.

Of the 17 validation-matrix cases:
- **One changes value:** MAT-B-01, B = 2 → 5, because the cap moves to A.
- **One has an ambiguous context:** MAT-A-02, "subalpin" must become cas 3 to keep A = 2; the lower subalpine gives A = 1.
- **Four need their region/stage context rewritten as a cas:** MAT-A-01, MAT-A-02, MAT-G-01 and the SUBMIT cases.

The research also found app bugs that diverge from both versions:
- G and H accept a score of 1; both versions allow only 0/2/5.
- The home screen and nearby-parcel badge show the 0–50 total as "/10", with colour thresholds 7 and 5.
- The `ibp_method_version` field required by the spec was never implemented.

**Primary recommendation:** D-06's "migrate" branch applies. ADR-003 should decide **Migrate to IBP FR v3.2, implemented in phase 01.8**:
- Submitted surveys keep their v3.0 scores and are tagged `ibp_method_version = v3.0`. Recomputing them is not meaningful: their raw counts were made under v3.0 field definitions.
- Drafts are re-scored under v3.2. The mechanical parts can be migrated: B's native-cover value moves to A, and the cas is derived where the stage is unambiguous.
- Until 01.8 lands, the citation files must say the app **implements v3.0** and **targets v3.2 (ADR-003)**. Otherwise criterion 5 ("cite the version the app actually implements") would be false.

## Classification model: v3.0 region/stage vs v3.2 "cas"

v3.2 p. 2 replaces the biogeographic-region model with four "cas" defined by growth constraints:
- **Cas 1:** low-to-medium constraints. This covers the temperate planitiaire, collinéen, montagnard and **lower subalpine** stages, plus well-watered riparian forests and humid supra-Mediterranean zones inside the Mediterranean region.
- **Cas 3:** high constraints from cold and a short growing season, i.e. the **middle and upper subalpine**.
- **Cas 4:** high constraints from heat and dry summers, i.e. the thermo-, meso- and supra-Mediterranean stages, except the cas-1 special cases.
- **Cas 2:** an overlay for very infertile stations, in any macroclimatic context. There, trees cannot reach the very-large-tree diameter: 67.5 cm in cas 1, 57.5 cm in cas 3/4. Heights generally stay ≤ 12 m (cas 1) or ≤ 8 m (cas 3/4). v3.2 calls this rare (p. 2, p. 5).

For factors A and G, cas-3 thresholds replace cas-2 ones in cas-3 zones and in forest habitats on lapiaz, dunes or peat bogs, or dominated by *Juniperus thurifera*. The footnote on p. 4 ties this to cas 2; p. 7 states it generally.

## Data model facts (D-07)

[VERIFIED: migrations, services and mobile storage read 2026-09-26]

**API (PostgreSQL) — the `surveys` table:**
- Stored per survey (`api/migrations/001_init.sql` l. 14–35; `002_factor_results.sql`):
  - `region_version TEXT`, `vegetation_stage TEXT`.
  - `factors JSONB`: the raw observer inputs as sent, e.g. `{"B":{"strata_count":5,"covered_autochthonous_percent":40}}`, or a bare number for a direct score.
  - `factor_results JSONB`: per factor `factor_id`, `observed_value_raw`, `selected_class`, `score_points`, `warnings`.
  - `scores JSONB`: `{ibp_peuplement_gestion, ibp_contexte, ibp_total}`.
- `version_number` (migration 008) is the **parcel follow-up version**, not a method version. **No method-version column exists.**
- The DTO restricts `region_version` to `ACA|M` (`survey-upsert.dto.ts` l. 61–63).

**When scores are computed:**
- On every upsert: `prepareUpsert` → `validateDraft` (`surveys.service.ts` l. 211–248). A failed validation returns 422 **before** the submitted-read-only check.
- On patch: l. 651–664.
- At submit: `validateSubmit`, l. 972–977.
- Submitted surveys are read-only for `region_version`, `vegetation_stage` and `factors`. An identical replay is accepted, but only `visibility`/`sync_version` are written; `scores`/`factor_results` are never rewritten (l. 380–399; `api-contract-v1.md` l. 786).

**Readers of the stored scores:**
- The public map, nearby parcels and parcel history read the stored `scores` (`public-map.queries.ts` l. 89–139, 199; `parcels.service.ts` l. 86–110).
- The public-map `region` filter matches `region_version` exactly (`api-contract-v1.md` l. 944).

**No recompute or backfill job exists.**

**Mobile (SQLite `cortege-local.db`):**
- `local_surveys.payload_json` holds the draft: `region_version`, `vegetation_stage`, `factors`, `parcel_ids`, `expires_at`, … (`mobile/src/storage/db.ts` l. 180–194). `payload_completion` is precomputed.
- The `sync_queue.payload` rows carry the same shape.
- Scores are **recomputed live** from the raw factors for the form and the local draft view (`useSurveyForm.ts` l. 250; `useLocalDraftSummary.ts` l. 64–86).
- `LEGACY_DEFAULT_FACTOR_VALUES` (`db.ts` l. 39–50) includes `B.covered_autochthonous_percent: 70`.

**Drafts expire 7 days after creation.** `expires_at` is set server-side and never moved by an upsert (`surveys.service.ts` l. 244–246). No v3.0 draft can outlive the switch-over by more than 7 days.

**What happens if the rules are changed in place, without versioning:**
- Submitted surveys keep their stored v3.0 scores, because nothing recomputes them.
- But a **replay** of a submitted v3.0 survey goes through `validateDraft` again. If the v3.2 engine requires a cas or an A native-cover input, those replays return 422. Installed apps replay pulled surveys, so their sync blocks. **Hence 01.8 must dispatch the rules by method version, with "no version" meaning v3.0.**

**Would stored scores change if recomputed under v3.2?**
- Deterministically, for the engine-level rules only:
  - B = 5 with native cover < 50 % → B becomes 5, and A drops to 2 when A was 5.
  - C/D/E with the lower class < 1/ha but lower + upper ≥ 1/ha → 0 becomes 1.
  - Subalpine surveys → the A/G scale depends on the subalpine level (unknown).
- Not at all for the field-definition changes. The raw counts (strata, types, dmh trees, genera, diameters) were made under v3.0 definitions and cannot be re-derived. **A v3.2 recomputation of a v3.0 survey would therefore be neither a v3.0 nor a v3.2 score.** This supports D-07's default: keep the submitted score and tag the version.

**Impact size is unknown from here.** Production data lives on the VPS. The planner should include a **read-only query** for the owner, so that ADR-003 can state numbers (see Code Examples).

## Decision (D-06) and draft ADR reasoning

**Branch:** v3.2 changes the scoring rules (four engine-level changes) and the Factor A genus list, so D-06's default applies: **migrate to IBP FR v3.2 in this milestone, implemented in phase 01.8**.

The "stay on v3.0" exception requires comparability that cannot be handled. It does not apply:
- Comparability is handled by tagging each survey with its method version and never mixing versions in one score.
- The milestone is internal-only (`PROJECT.md` l. 11), so the population of recorded surveys is small field-test data ([ASSUMED] until the query runs).
- The 7-day draft expiry bounds the transition.

**Draft ADR-003 content (the executor turns this into the ADR):**
- **Status:** Accepted (autonomous; the owner reviews at phase end). **Date:** 2026-09-26.
- **Context:**
  - The repo cites Fr v3.0 (links now 404); CNPF publishes FR v3.2 (02/02/2026). Phase 1 derived the genus list from v3.2.
  - The comparison doc lists 4 engine-level and ~21 field-definition differences.
  - The app also carries a v3.0-era bug: the cover cap is on B instead of A.
- **Decision:** "Migrate the app to IBP FR v3.2. The rules change once, in `packages/ibp-domain`, in phase 01.8, against the change list in the comparison document. Until 01.8 ships, the app computes Fr v3.0 scores and the documentation says so."
- **Effect on recorded surveys (D-07):**
  - Submitted surveys keep the score they were submitted with and are tagged `cnpf_ibp_fr_v3_0_2023-03-23`. They are not recomputed: their raw counts follow v3.0 definitions, so a recomputation would be neither version.
  - Drafts are re-scored under v3.2 when opened after the switch, using mechanical migration where possible:
    - B's `covered_autochthonous_percent` becomes A's native cover (it is the same quantity: the share of native species in the stand's cover).
    - The cas is derived from region/stage where unambiguous (see the mapping table).
    - Where the stage is ambiguous (`subalpin`, and optionally `supra_mediterraneen`), the cas is left blank and blocks submit until the observer picks it.
  - Because drafts expire after 7 days, this affects at most one week of drafts.
  - New surveys are tagged `cnpf_ibp_fr_v3_2_2026-02-02`.
  - Anything that shows or averages scores (public map, nearby-parcel average, parcel history) must either show the version or not mix versions in one average. 01.8 decides which.
- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - (a) Stay on v3.0: the documents are no longer published or retrievable, observers are trained on v3.2, and the phase-2 genus list would come from v3.2 while the app scored v3.0.
  - (b) Recompute all history under v3.2: impossible for field-definition changes, and misleading.
  - (c) Migrate now in this phase: out of scope; 01.8 owns the rules package.
- **Consequences:**
  - A method-version field is needed (01.8 or phase 2, per CONTEXT deferred list).
  - `region_version`/`vegetation_stage` give way to a cas input; contracts change (`api-contract-v1.md`, `data-contract-v1.md` l. 54–55).
  - The validation matrix becomes v2.
  - Phase 2 builds the genus list from v3.2 Table 1, keyed by cas.
- **Open points for CNPF:**
  - Ficus: in Table 1 but not in the genus list.
  - Pistacia: supplementary genus with only shrub species.
  - The linear C/D table still uses BMm alone.

**Criterion 5 wording (important):** after this phase, the code still implements v3.0. `docs/references/README.md` and `docs/specs/ibp-form-spec.md` must therefore say, in substance: "Implemented: IBP Fr v3.0 (as transcribed in this spec; the official v3.0 PDFs are no longer online). Target: IBP FR v3.2 (02/02/2026), adopted by ADR-003, lands in phase 01.8." They should add the v3.2 URL and record the dead v3.0 links as dead (not delete them silently). 01.8 flips "implemented" to v3.2. Writing "the app implements v3.2" now would falsify criterion 5.

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: Criterion 5 made false by an optimistic citation
**What goes wrong:** the README/spec say "v3.2" while the code still runs v3.0.
**How to avoid:** use the wording "implemented v3.0 / target v3.2 (ADR-003, 01.8)".
**Warning signs:** `grep -n "v3.2" docs/references/README.md` shows v3.2 without the word "target" or "ADR-003".

### Pitfall 2: Replay breakage when rules change
**What goes wrong:** `prepareUpsert` re-validates every upsert, including identical replays of submitted surveys. A stricter v3.2 engine then 422s old surveys and blocks device sync.
**How to avoid:** the ADR's consequences and CH-6 require version dispatch, with legacy meaning v3.0.

### Pitfall 3: Page numbers off by one
**What goes wrong:** the spike doc already mis-cites v3.2 pages.
**How to avoid:** cite the printed "p. N" header. Genus list p. 3 (and p. 20); Table 1 p. 10–11; Table 2 p. 11; cas definitions p. 2; complements p. 5–9.

### Pitfall 4: Over-quoting
**What goes wrong:** D-03 is breached by copying the typology descriptions.
**How to avoid:** at most short quoted phrases (e.g. « Score plafonné à 2 », « Mer ou océan ») with page references.

### Pitfall 5: Counting Ficus or shrub congeners in the genus list
**What goes wrong:** Table 1 includes Ficus, but the genus list does not; Table 2 species never count.
**How to avoid:** state the rule (p. 6): only listed genera, restricted to Table 1 species native at the location.

### Pitfall 6: Forgetting that I/J/G/H scores are 0/2/5
**What goes wrong:** the matrix Notes say "A..H: 0|1|2|5".
**How to avoid:** the comparison records G and H as 0/2/5 in both versions (BUG-2).

## Code Examples

### Read-only impact query for the owner (VPS, for ADR-003's D-07 numbers)
```sql
-- Source: derived from api/migrations/001_init.sql column shapes; factors may hold a bare number (direct score)
WITH s AS (
  SELECT status, vegetation_stage, factors,
    CASE WHEN jsonb_typeof(factors->'B') = 'object' THEN factors->'B' END AS b,
    CASE WHEN jsonb_typeof(factors->'C') = 'object' THEN factors->'C' END AS c,
    CASE WHEN jsonb_typeof(factors->'D') = 'object' THEN factors->'D' END AS d,
    CASE WHEN jsonb_typeof(factors->'E') = 'object' THEN factors->'E' END AS e
  FROM surveys WHERE deleted_at IS NULL
)
SELECT status,
  count(*) AS surveys,
  count(*) FILTER (WHERE (b->>'strata_count')::numeric >= 5
    AND COALESCE(b->>'covered_autochthonous_percent', b->>'native_cover_percent')::numeric < 50) AS b_cap_hits,
  count(*) FILTER (WHERE (c->>'bmg_count')::numeric / NULLIF((c->>'surface_ha')::numeric,0) < 1
    AND (c->>'bmm_count')::numeric / NULLIF((c->>'surface_ha')::numeric,0) < 1
    AND ((c->>'bmg_count')::numeric + (c->>'bmm_count')::numeric) / NULLIF((c->>'surface_ha')::numeric,0) >= 1) AS c_sum_rule_hits,
  count(*) FILTER (WHERE (d->>'bmg_count')::numeric / NULLIF((d->>'surface_ha')::numeric,0) < 1
    AND (d->>'bmm_count')::numeric / NULLIF((d->>'surface_ha')::numeric,0) < 1
    AND ((d->>'bmg_count')::numeric + (d->>'bmm_count')::numeric) / NULLIF((d->>'surface_ha')::numeric,0) >= 1) AS d_sum_rule_hits,
  count(*) FILTER (WHERE (e->>'tgb_count')::numeric / NULLIF((e->>'surface_ha')::numeric,0) < 1
    AND (e->>'gb_count')::numeric / NULLIF((e->>'surface_ha')::numeric,0) < 1
    AND ((e->>'tgb_count')::numeric + (e->>'gb_count')::numeric) / NULLIF((e->>'surface_ha')::numeric,0) >= 1) AS e_sum_rule_hits,
  count(*) FILTER (WHERE vegetation_stage = 'subalpin') AS subalpine,
  count(*) FILTER (WHERE factors->>'G' = '1' OR factors->>'H' = '1'
    OR factors->'H'->>'class_score' = '1') AS g_h_score_1
FROM s GROUP BY status;
```
Values are stored as JSON numbers by the mobile app. If a cast fails on a string value, wrap the casts in a `~ '^[0-9.]+$'` guard. This is a task for the owner; the executor cannot reach the VPS.
