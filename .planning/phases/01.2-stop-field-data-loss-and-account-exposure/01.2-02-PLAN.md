| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01.2-06 | Spoofing | AuthGuard.getOrProvisionUser email linking (A-H1) | mitigate | link only when `email_verified === true`; unverified + existing email → 401 with no write; unit + E2E cases |
| T-01.2-07 | Tampering | Concurrent first-login provisioning | mitigate | `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT (auth0_sub)` plus 23505 re-select; E2E fires 5 concurrent calls and asserts one row |
| T-01.2-08 | Information Disclosure | `reported` survey_events payload/actor_id read by owner via getEvents and /sync/changes (A-M6) | mitigate | stop writing actor_id/reason; migration 013 scrubs history; E2E asserts owner feed is redacted |
| T-01.2-09 | Denial of Service | Unbounded report reason | mitigate | `@MaxLength(2000)` on CreateReportDto.reason; E2E asserts 400 at 2001 chars |
| T-01.2-10 | Repudiation | Removing actor from event | accept | reporter identity remains in `reports.reporter_user_id`, readable by moderators only |
