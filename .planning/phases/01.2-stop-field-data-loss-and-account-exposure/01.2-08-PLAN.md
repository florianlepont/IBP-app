| User intent → destructive purge | Deletion of unsynced field data requires an explicit, counted confirmation |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-01.2-28 | Information Disclosure | User-triggered senders (submit, visibility, delete) during conflict | mitigate | blocking full-screen overlay while status is "conflict"; only two actions exposed |
| T-01.2-29 | Denial of Service (data loss) | Logout purges unsynced work silently | mitigate | countUnsyncedLocalWork + Alert with count; purge only in destructive onPress; tests assert no purge before onPress |
| T-01.2-30 | Denial of Service (data loss) | Conflict "delete" pressed by mistake | mitigate | second confirmation Alert with the count and "irréversible"; discardForeignData only in its destructive onPress |
