# Reference material

The IBP methodology documents and the Etats Sauvages graphic charter are **not
redistributed in this repository**. They are third-party works and remain the
property of their respective authors.

## IBP methodology — CNPF / INRAE Dynafor

Authors: Gonin P., Larrieu L. — © CNPF, INRAE Dynafor.
All official documents are published on the CNPF website:

- IBP home page (all documents): <https://www.cnpf.fr/ibp>
- IBP Fr v3.0 — definition: <https://www.cnpf.fr/sites/socle/files/cnpf-old/medias/documents/9a66d6016d0a99f576f35f53df4e73f3/ibp_def_fr_v3_0_230323_0.pdf>
- IBP Fr v3.0 — survey sheets: <https://www.cnpf.fr/sites/socle/files/cnpf-old/medias/documents/5cf710f876f8e4ddfd4007df318f71f5/ibp_rel_fr_v3_0_230323_0.pdf>
- IBP — survey methods: <https://www.cnpf.fr/sites/socle/files/cnpf-old/medias/documents/e5f7f1ea0f6f4f63a2ef41a58ecab8e0/ibp_methodes_de_releve_v221010_0.pdf>

Cite as: Gonin P., Larrieu L., 2022 — *Indice de Biodiversité Potentielle
(IBP Fr v3.0)*. CNPF, INRAE Dynafor.

The scoring rules implemented in this app are derived from these documents. The
factor-by-factor transcription used by the product lives in
[`../specs/ibp-form-spec.md`](../specs/ibp-form-spec.md) and
[`../technical/ibp-validation-matrix-v1.md`](../technical/ibp-validation-matrix-v1.md).
