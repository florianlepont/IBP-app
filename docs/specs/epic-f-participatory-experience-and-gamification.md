# Epic F - Participatory Experience and Gamification

**Release:** V1

Drive engagement through visibility, points, rankings, progression, and community participation.

---

## User Stories

### US-F1 - France-wide IBP Map

**Release:** MVP

View completed public IBP surveys on a map of France, with the ability to filter by date range and region, while ensuring sensitive data is not exposed.

As a contributor, I want to view completed IBP surveys on a map of France so I can track collective progress.

Acceptance criteria

- I can open a map showing completed `public` IBP surveys in French forests.

- I can filter by date range and region/department.

- Sensitive data is not exposed (no exact private personal information).

### US-F2 - Contributor Profile and Points

**Release:** V1

Contributor profiles will display total points, validated IBP surveys, and activity history, with points updating automatically upon validation or submission of surveys, while drafts or rejected surveys do not earn points.

As a contributor, I want to see my personal score so I can track my contribution.

Acceptance criteria

- My profile displays total points, number of validated IBP surveys, and activity history.

- Points update automatically after a survey is validated/submitted according to rules.

- Draft or rejected surveys do not grant points.

### US-F3 - Leaderboard

**Release:** V1

A leaderboard will be created to allow contributors to compare their contributions, with options to filter rankings by national and regional scope and by time periods (all-time, month, week). Users can select a display name while keeping their personal identity protected.

As a contributor, I want to see a leaderboard so I can compare my contribution with others.

Acceptance criteria

- A leaderboard is available at least by national and regional scope.

- Ranking can be filtered by period (all-time, month, week).

- Users can choose a display name; personal identity remains protected.

### US-F4 - Badges and Milestones

**Release:** V1

Badges and milestones are to be awarded for submitting surveys, with clear criteria for earning them, including milestones for the first, tenth, and fiftieth surveys. Newly earned badges should be prominently displayed, and the rules for earning badges must be transparent and accessible within the app.

As a contributor, I want to unlock badges/milestones so I stay motivated to submit more IBP surveys.

Acceptance criteria

- Badges are awarded for clear milestones (first survey, 10 surveys, 50 surveys, etc.).

- Newly earned badges are clearly highlighted.

- Badge rules are transparent and visible in the app.

### US-F5 - Earn points and badges for scanning rare tree species

**Release:** V1

Field surveyors can earn points for scanning rare tree species and unlock badges for each species scanned. A reference list will manage which species are considered rare, and users can view their earned points and badges in their profile. Anti-abuse rules will limit scoring scans to one per parcel per day for each species.

As a field surveyor, I want to earn points when I scan (identify) rare tree species, and unlock badges per tree species, so that participation is more motivating.

Acceptance criteria

- A species can be marked as “rare” (managed by the team via a reference list).

- When a rare species is confirmed from a scan, points are awarded.

- Badges exist per species (or per set of species) and are unlocked when the species is scanned.

- A user can view earned points and unlocked badges in their profile.

- Anti-abuse rules are defined (for example: one scoring scan per parcel per day for a given species).
